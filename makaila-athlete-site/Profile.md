# **Makaila Schneider – Softball Recruiting Profile**

**Makaila Schneider** is a collegiate softball player from Chilliwack, British Columbia, currently an infielder for the Salt Lake Community College (SLCC) Bruins softball team. As a **5’3”** right-handed batter/thrower, she completed her freshman season at Salt Lake Community College (SLCC) in 2025. Schneider is a graduate of **Yale Secondary** (Class of 2024) and was an honors student throughout high school, demonstrating the same dedication in the classroom as on the field. She brings multi-position versatility, having played second base, third base, shortstop, and outfield in her youth and travel teams, including the White Rock Renegades and Langley Fusion in British Columbia, Canada.

## **Academic Achievements**

* **High School:** Yale Secondary (Abbotsford, BC) – Honors student (Class of 2024). Consistently on the Honor Roll each year of high school, reflecting a strong work ethic and academic commitment. 

* **Intended Major:** Nursing.

* **Academic Awards:** Honor Roll every semester in high school. Plans to pursue NJCAA Academic All-Region honors at SLCC with continued strong academic performance.

## **Notable Accomplishments & Highlights**

* **Clutch Hitting:** Made an immediate impact in her first collegiate season. In her home conference debut, Schneider hit a pivotal two-run home run to help SLCC to a 9–8 victory over CSI. In the Region 18 Tournament opener, she also **“went deep”** with a solo home run and added a double, totaling 3 RBIs in a 13–4 win over Snow College. These performances highlight her ability to deliver under pressure in postseason play.

* 2025 season, last minor ball season post freshmen year. Makaila played for Fusion 2006 where they earned gold in the Canadian U19 Nationals. She was a big part in the team win and played 2nd base and 3rd base during the whole week at nationals fielding 1.000% in both provincials and nationals. 

* **Youth & Travel Ball:** As a member of the White Rock Renegades ’06 team, Schneider helped her squad earn a bronze medal at the 2022 Canadian U17 Fastpitch Nationals. In a key national tournament game, **she led the offense with three hits and a run scored**, showcasing her talent against elite competition. She also competed at the club level for the Ai Bandits (16U) and the White Rock Renegades, where she developed a reputation as a versatile utility player (INF/OF) with leadership on and off the field.

* **Leadership & Work ethic: Makaila is k**nown for her high energy and determined work ethic in both the weight room and field. Coaches have praised her for her accountability, effort, attitude and motivation. She is extremely passionate and will do anything to improve, she is extremely coachable!
